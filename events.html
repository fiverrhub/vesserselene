Submission Requirements

Entries should follow the requirements below to remain eligible for judging.

Novel Requirements

  • The submitted work must be an original novel written by the participant.
  • Novel length must be between 20,000 and 80,000 words.
  • Only one novel submission is permitted per participant.
  • The manuscript must be complete and submitted before the deadline.
  • Any genre is welcome unless otherwise stated in final guidelines.
  • The work must not contain plagiarized material.

Format & Submission

  • Submission window: August 22–September 22, 2026.
  • Recommended manuscript formats: DOCX or PDF.
  • Use a clear, readable font and consistent chapter formatting.
  • Include the novel title and participant name with the submission.
  • Late or incomplete submissions may not be accepted.
  • Final upload instructions will be provided to confirmed participants.

Judging Criteria

Entries will be evaluated on the overall quality and impact of the novel.

What Judges Look For

  • Originality: Fresh ideas and creative execution.
  • Storytelling: A compelling plot with effective pacing.
  • Characters: Memorable, believable, well-developed characters.
  • Writing Quality: Strong language, clarity, and grammar.
  • World & Atmosphere: Effective setting, tone, and imagery.
  • Overall Impact: Emotional resonance of the full read.

Important Conditions

  • Participants are responsible for meeting the word-count requirement.
  • Submissions must be received during the official period.
  • Entries may be disqualified for plagiarism or rule violations.
  • Participants should retain their own copy of the manuscript.
  • Full judging procedures may be shared separately with confirmed participants.
